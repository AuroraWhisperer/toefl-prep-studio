# TOEFL Practice Workbench

## World

The interface treats language as a field of ink on a test sheet. The homepage keeps its editorial ink, paper, and blue identity. Inside the workbench, warm charcoal carries the reading, paper white keeps long passages calm, and a restrained eucalyptus green marks actions and selection. The page feels like an instrument for concentrated practice rather than a marketing site.

## Tokens

Styles load through explicit links in `frontend/index.html`. `styles.css` owns shared tokens, typography, controls and shell rules; `landing.css` owns homepage cards; `practice.css` owns the question sheet and shared question material/audio controls used in review; `practice-setup.css` owns practice configuration; `practice-review.css` owns results and material review. Each file keeps its responsive overrides with its owning rules. Mock, adaptive-test and history styles retain their existing files and load after these shared styles. This split preserves the current visual design; it adds no build step.

The homepage retains its existing local tokens. Setup, practice, results, mock exams, and history share these scoped roles in `frontend/styles.css`:

- Reading / headings: warm charcoal `#303B37`
- Canvas / content: paper `#F7F7F2` / white `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary surfaces: `#EDF0E9`
- Secondary text: `#606D66`
- Primary action / links / selection / focus: eucalyptus `#28675D`
- Primary action hover: deeper eucalyptus `#1D5149`, not black
- Selected surface / supporting audio action: pale sage `#E8F1EC`
- Structural rule / secondary-control border: `#D4DBD3` / `#7D8D84`
- Correct result: olive green `#356343` on `#EDF4E8`
- Incorrect result / recording / destructive action: terracotta `#AA443B` on `#FBEFEC`
- Body: system sans for controls and reading copy; compact monospace only for question IDs and timers.

Primary actions use a solid accent; secondary controls use neutral outlines, and return/utility text remains neutral. Audio playback uses a lighter accent surface so it does not compete with navigation. Selection retains native radio marks or explicit checks and does not imply correctness; result labels and recording text preserve non-color cues. Do not introduce per-subject palettes or change the homepage when adjusting internal tokens.

Color-role guidance follows [Atlassian's color foundations](https://atlassian.design/foundations/color): distinguish neutral, action, selection, and semantic roles rather than color every label. Check rendered text at 4.5:1 and essential control/focus indicators at 3:1, following [WCAG text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). `tests/browser_palette.test.cjs` covers computed colors, hover/focus, status contrast, homepage isolation, and desktop scaling.

## Surface

This is a desktop-only product. New work is judged at desktop window sizes and Windows display scaling; existing narrow-window fallbacks remain safeguards, not a separate mobile experience.

The shared task-practice setup uses a compact subject-tab bar followed by a section heading and one bordered white settings sheet. Task types are large radio choices; quantity and timing use compact segmented groups with native radio semantics. A ruled footer places the live quantity/time summary on the left and the primary start action on the right. Listening and Speaking keep the full voice/accent disclaimer in a labeled, initially collapsed disclosure; practice instructions remain a separate disclosure below the sheet. The shared internal color roles and visible keyboard focus are preserved across all four subjects.

The homepage opens with a 36px book mark at the upper left and a compact TOEFL TRAINING wordmark: TOEFL uses a locally hosted Cormorant Garamond semibold italic subset, while TRAINING and all controls retain the sans-serif face. The font license is stored alongside the asset in `frontend/fonts/`; no font service is contacted at runtime. The question-bank total is omitted. Its seven entry cards retain their editorial board: a two-by-two subject grid on the left with a 16px stagger, a blue mock-paper ticket above a quiet adaptive-test card on the right, and a horizontal Learning Archive card beneath them. Desktop cards expand with viewport height: gutters grow from 20px to 28px, padding from 24px to 32px, and subject-card minimum heights from 216px to 340px. Headings and descriptions are slightly larger in windows at least 800px tall. The extra space goes into cards and separation rather than a large empty footer, while all seven entries and their actions remain in one desktop viewport, including 1280×720 and the usable browser areas at 125% and 150% display scaling on the target monitor. Short or narrow windows may scroll naturally; content is never clipped to force a fit. Decorative frames use book-cover double rules, a sound-wave edge, manuscript crop marks, a quotation frame, ticket perforations, a dashed reservation frame, and an archive folder tab. Frames do not receive pointer events; narrow-window fallbacks remove the stagger. The original paper, ink, and blue surfaces remain in use.

Card copy describes each subject's distinct tasks without repeating counts or a generic training slogan. The archive leads to a ruled, chronological list rather than nested cards. Its workspace uses the available desktop width with 24–80px side gutters, a compact title and return action, and one white bordered list surface. Category tabs and date filters share a toolbar on wide windows and wrap below 1100px. Each row leads with the practice title, followed by completion time, the original score label, and a review action; a compact total replaces repeated category and per-page counts. The archive omits the repeated English subtitle and introductory explanation. Programmatic heading focus remains available to assistive technology without a decorative outline; interactive controls retain visible keyboard focus. Practice, Mock, and Test tabs separate completed submissions, date filters use local calendar days, and ten-row pages keep large histories navigable. Reopening a record reuses the original answer review; returning preserves its category, dates, and page. Loading, saved-test resume, empty dates, network errors, and recording-upload retry are explicit states.

The homepage separates four subject practice cards on the left from ETS mock papers and the active comprehensive-test entry on the right. The mock-paper picker expands below the cards and lists the five ETS forms; comprehensive tests open their own difficulty setup. Mock papers have their own device check, fixed nine-stage flow, untimed Directions screens, native per-question reading controls and sentence word tiles, forward-only listening and speaking, and final review. These screens use the shared internal color roles. The fixed single-section action is labeled 整科练习 rather than 单科模拟 to avoid confusing it with the five ETS papers.

The practice cards show section names, question counts, task practice and fixed whole-section entry points. The whole card opens task practice through the primary button's extended hit area; the whole-section button has its own hit area and both actions support keyboard navigation. Detailed task descriptions stay in the tabbed setup, which presents 12 task choices across four sections with quantity and timing controls. The practice view puts the title and timer in one compact top bar, followed immediately by the question sheet. Rules, repeated task headings, timing guidance, and skill descriptions are omitted during practice. A single step hides the question rail and previous button; multiple steps retain the rail. Missing-letter inputs sit directly in the passage, and answer progress shares the bottom navigation band with submission. The layout prioritizes fitting the full question and controls in one desktop viewport while allowing long content to scroll. Review groups answers, references, explanations, and time in the left pane and original materials, prompts, and options in the right pane; the panes stack in narrow desktop windows, and missing-letter passages can reveal correct answers. The signature interaction is the text field of the question number: completed items become inked marks in the rail, and the current item is outlined in signal blue.

The result view combines its title, score, answered count, and elapsed time into one compact header. Single-group review hides the material navigation. Missing-letter review uses a narrow answer column with one line per blank; correct words emphasize only the missing letters. The passage title and answer-reveal control share one row, and explanations are collapsed until requested.

Expanded learning explanations preserve three short text lines: 读懂, 解析, and 下次. Plain Chinese explains the necessary English evidence, with an actionable next-time method rather than another paraphrase of the answer. The existing disclosure and typography remain in use, without extra cards or repeated panels. Completed mock review labels its learning notes as locally authored rather than ETS explanations.

After submission, multiple-choice review navigation uses green for fully correct material groups and red for groups containing a wrong or unanswered question. The active group keeps an inset underline without replacing its result color, and accessible labels and tooltips describe the status. Correct options are green; a selected wrong option is red; other options stay neutral. These states also apply when reopening saved practice history; pre-submission selection remains blue.

The archive header keeps destructive actions behind a compact 记录管理 disclosure, separate from date filters and review links. Its actions are 重置概率 and 全部清空, with short descriptions explaining what is retained or deleted. Both use the existing native confirmation pattern; the destructive action is red and names its all-category scope and permanence in the confirmation. Pending actions are disabled, while completion and retryable errors appear above the list. The full-clear action resets the current page and date filters; category selection is retained.

## Motion and states

The comprehensive-test entry is active, with a checklist icon and “选择难度开始” action. Its setup reuses the workbench typography, sage selection and visible keyboard focus: five preset buttons, a labeled native 1–10 select, the linked routing range and one start action. Directions separate untimed instructions from each server-timed stage. Reuse the practice task renderer; show current module and local target level in the title, and lock submitted modules. Completed Test review displays the starting level, two routing outcomes and objective counts; open responses say “待人工复核”, not a fabricated zero score. See [adaptive test behavior](specs/adaptive-tests.md).

Complete the Words passage review keeps one separate underline per missing letter, matching the practice input spacing. Unfilled positions remain empty underlined slots; switching between submitted and correct answers preserves the slots and the existing correct/incorrect colors.

Build a Sentence practice follows the screen example in the ETS overview: context, word bank, then the inline answer frame. The frame has solid underline blanks and immutable fixed words or punctuation; used tiles remain visible but disabled. Empty slots have accessible numbered labels without visible numbers. Click, keyboard, drag, undo and reset preserve individual tile identity. Mock sentence slots retain their separate rendering, exam state and persistence.

Practice uses task-specific layouts rather than a universal two-column template. Listening keeps audio on the left and the question with vertically ordered single-answer options on the right. Daily-life and academic reading use balanced material/question columns; academic passages scroll independently with keyboard access on wide desktops. Complete the Words keeps all ten gaps in the paragraph beneath a task instruction. Email places the existing task on the left and the editor on the right. Academic Discussion keeps the professor's question and instructions on the left, with the two existing student posts above the editor on the right; an unrecognized prompt format stays intact instead of losing content. Both writing editors keep the live word count in their header. Build a Sentence follows a vertical flow up to 1100px wide. Speaking centers listening and recording in one primary area, with response transcription in a collapsed practice aid below; saved transcriptions reopen, and microphone failure exposes the manual fallback. Interview questions remain available through the existing collapsed audio script, rather than as a permanent reading prompt. The interface does not fabricate speaker photos, interviewer video, email addresses, or subject lines; recipient and subject text authored in a task is displayed with its instructions. Below 1100px, side-by-side areas stack and passage scrolling returns to the page. See [official task-layout evidence and limitations](docs/ui-task-layouts.md).

Audio controls sit on a light, ruled toolbar rather than a shaded block. Answer options are white, softly rounded rows with boxed letter markers and a reserved selection indicator. Hover uses quiet paper, selection uses pale sage with an explicit eucalyptus check, and keyboard focus has a separate ink outline. These states reuse the existing single-answer behavior; selection does not imply correctness. Recording controls and word counts use spacing rather than nested gray panels.

Practice and review audio scripts use the full audio-panel width rather than a character-width cap. Each dialogue turn is its own paragraph with a bold speaker label and the existing paragraph spacing; monologues keep their existing line breaks. Scripts remain collapsed during practice and available in review. Practice replay, scripts, and transcription are labeled as local learning aids; their presence must not imply that these controls appear in the actual TOEFL exam.

The primary desktop target is 2560×1440, including Windows display scaling. Wide viewports use an 18px base size and a 1760px maximum work area, keeping reading and review in two columns. Question navigation stays at the bottom while scrolling long material. Homepage cards place their two practice actions side by side and use pointer-position tilt capped at 4 degrees on hover-capable mouse devices. Cards settle on exit, navigation, scroll, or focus loss; touch and reduced-motion users receive a static layout.

A question sheet enters with a short upward settle; reduced motion disables it. Audio, recording, loading, empty, error, and submitted states are all visible in text. Internal focus rings use the action color (or a distinct ink outline on answer options), with a visible outline and offset rather than color alone; the homepage retains blue focus.
